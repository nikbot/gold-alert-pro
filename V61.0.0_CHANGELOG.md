# Gold Alert Pro v61.0.0

## New
- AI Decision Room with regime, trend, signal, risk and three market scenarios.
- AI plain-language decision explanation endpoint (`/api/ai-decision`) with rule-engine fallback.
- Smart Alerts combining price, trend, signal, score, RSI, pressure and recent change.
- Server-side smart alert persistence and push/notification delivery.
- Portfolio Guard with keep/reduce/add scenario allocation.
- Premium responsive UI for Decision Room, Smart Alerts and Portfolio Guard.
- Updated service worker/cache version.

## Safety / behavior
- Advanced features still require account authentication; the public dashboard remains available without login.
- AI output is scenario-based and explicitly non-guaranteed.
- Smart alerts are rate-limited and persisted on the server.
